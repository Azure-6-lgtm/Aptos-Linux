Why Choose Nixel

Built for Performance & Ease of Use

Everything you want from Arch Linux, without the setup headaches.

Built on Arch

A solid, well-known foundation giving you full access to Arch repositories and the AUR.

Blazing Fast

Zero bloatware. Boots in seconds with an ultra-light idle RAM footprint under 1000MB.

Bleeding-Edge Rolling

Always receive the newest software releases and kernels as soon as they drop without re-installing.

Beginner Friendly

Pre-configured environment with installers and guided setups, no wiki required to start.

Rock-Solid Stability

Automated Btrfs system snapshots ensure seamless rollbacks if updates ever break a configuration.

100% Open Source
